  <section class="project-section">
    <h2>Overview</h2>

    <p>
      I joined Purdue’s Engineering Projects in Community Service (EPICS)
      program to help design and construct a roofing material storage unit
      for the nonprofit Habitat for Humanity. I worked with a team of five
      through the design process to meet specifications, conceptually design,
      and manufacture the storage system.
    </p>
  </section>


  <section class="project-section">
    <h2>Project Specifications</h2>

    <p>
      My team and I visited Habitat for Humanity’s site in Lafayette, IN to
      understand our project and the constraints around it. We developed
      project specifications and laid out a rough timeline over the course
      of the Fall 2025 and Spring 2026 semesters.
    </p>

  <section class="project-section">
    <h2>Concept Design</h2>

    <p>
      I prioritized functionality for my conceptual designs, focusing on
      safety, cost, and meeting the project criteria. After exchanging ideas
      with my team and receiving feedback from our client, we settled on a
      two unit, two shelf design. Each unit would have three plywood walls
      and a slanted roof, with the shelves fastened to the support beams using
      through bolts. I calculated that each shelf could sustain the weight of
      800 lbs with a safety factor of 1.25.
    </p>

  <section class="project-section">
    <h2>Manufacturing</h2>

    <p>
      I purchased and picked up materials with my team, and spent most of the
      Spring 2026 semester building the storage units. I learned new skills in
      the woodshop and verified my weight calculations on the physical shelves.
      We didn’t get to the sidewalls of the storage system or putting all the
      parts together, but we left the project at a good place for next semester’s team.
    </p>
